
# DOC-001 — Enterprise Knowledge Access Policy

**Organization:** Northstar Demo Manufacturing  
**Document Type:** Policy  
**Classification:** Internal Training  
**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

Define how employees may access and use approved enterprise information.

## Access Requirements

Employees must authenticate before accessing the enterprise knowledge system.

Access to documents must be based on the employee's assigned permissions.

The AI assistant must not retrieve or disclose information that the requesting employee is not authorized to access.

## Approved Information Sources

The assistant may retrieve information only from knowledge sources approved for the requesting employee.

Unapproved personal files, private employee records, and restricted business information must not be included in search results for unauthorized users.

## AI Assistant Restrictions

The assistant must not bypass access controls.

Access to a document does not automatically grant permission to modify the system or process described in that document.

## Escalation

Requests for additional information access must be reviewed through the organization's established access-request process.
