
# DOC-006 — Maintenance Communication Policy

**Organization:** Northstar Demo Manufacturing  
**Document Type:** Policy  
**Classification:** Internal Training  
**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

Define communication requirements for scheduled maintenance activities affecting enterprise systems.

## Maintenance Notification

Scheduled maintenance that may affect system availability must be communicated to affected personnel before the maintenance begins.

The notification must identify:

- The affected system.
- The planned maintenance window.
- The expected operational impact.
- The responsible support team.
- The contact method for reporting problems.

## Maintenance Approval

Maintenance activities must be approved through the organization's established change-management process.

An AI assistant may explain an approved maintenance procedure but must not independently authorize or execute a maintenance activity.

## Completion Notification

Affected personnel must receive a completion notification when the maintenance activity has concluded.

Any unresolved issues must be documented and escalated through the appropriate support process.
