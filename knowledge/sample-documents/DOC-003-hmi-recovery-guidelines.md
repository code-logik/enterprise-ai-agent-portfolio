
# DOC-003 — HMI Recovery Guidelines

**Organization:** Northstar Demo Manufacturing  
**Document Type:** Technical Guideline  
**Classification:** Internal Training  
**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

Define the initial reporting and escalation process when a Human-Machine Interface (HMI) experiences an unexpected interruption.

## Initial Assessment

When an HMI becomes unavailable, the operator must report the interruption to the designated operations or engineering support team.

The operator must record:

- The affected HMI.
- The approximate time of the interruption.
- Any visible alarms or error messages.
- Whether communication with the PLC appears to be unavailable.
- Whether the interruption affects production operations.

## Recovery Authorization

HMI recovery actions must follow the applicable approved equipment-specific procedure.

An AI assistant must not independently initiate an HMI restart, modify PLC values, or change equipment configuration.

## Escalation

If the HMI remains unavailable or PLC communication cannot be confirmed, the incident must be escalated to authorized technical personnel.

The incident must be documented according to DOC-002, the Technical Incident Reporting Procedure.
