
# DOC-002 — Technical Incident Reporting Procedure

**Organization:** Northstar Demo Manufacturing  
**Document Type:** Procedure  
**Classification:** Internal Training  
**Version:** 1.0

## Purpose

Define the information employees must record when reporting technical incidents.

## Incident Information

An incident report must include:

1. Date and time of the incident.
2. Affected equipment or system.
3. Description of the observed problem.
4. Relevant error messages or alarms.
5. Operational impact.
6. Actions already performed.
7. Current equipment or system status.

## Reporting Procedure

The employee must create a support ticket using the approved incident-reporting system.

The ticket must include the required incident information and any relevant supporting documentation.

If the affected equipment is part of a production process, the employee must identify that fact in the report.

## Escalation

Incidents affecting production availability must be escalated to the designated operations or engineering support team.

Employees must not perform corrective actions outside their assigned authority.
