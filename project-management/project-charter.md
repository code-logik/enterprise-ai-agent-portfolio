# Project Charter

## Project

Secure Enterprise AI Assistant

## Business Problem

Employees often spend significant time locating information
across company documentation, procedures, technical references,
and other internal knowledge sources.

The organization needs a secure AI assistant capable of locating
relevant enterprise information and explaining answers clearly.

## Objective

Develop an enterprise AI assistant that can:

- Search approved enterprise information sources.
- Answer employee questions.
- Provide citations showing where answers came from.
- Respect security and access-control boundaries.
- Eventually perform explicitly authorized actions.

## Users

Primary users may include:

- IT personnel
- Engineers
- Operations personnel
- Management

## Success Criteria

The system should:

- Retrieve relevant information reliably.
- Provide sources for important answers.
- Avoid exposing information users are not authorized to access.
- Require authorization for actions that change systems or data.
